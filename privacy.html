Website usage

This is a static information website. It does not provide user accounts, an online booking system, a payment facility or a contact form. The website files do not ask you to enter personal information. Your analytics choice is stored in your browser so the site can remember it.

When the site is placed on a web host, that hosting provider may process basic technical request information—such as an IP address, request time, requested page and browser details—to deliver and secure the website. The exact provider and its log-retention period depend on the hosting service selected before launch.

Any necessary technical information would be used only to operate, protect and troubleshoot the website. It is not sold or used by James Hand Carwash for advertising.

Calls and contact links

Selecting a telephone link asks your phone or device to start a call. The website itself does not receive information simply because you select that link.

If you call James Hand Carwash, the business may receive your telephone number and any information you choose to provide. That information is used to answer your enquiry or arrange the service you request and should be kept only for as long as it is needed for that purpose, subject to any legal record-keeping duties.

The WhatsApp link opens a conversation with a suggested message. The message is not sent unless you choose to send it. WhatsApp handles your visit and any message under its own privacy terms.

Google reviews, links and maps

Public review excerpts

The website displays selected excerpts from reviews posted publicly on the James Hand Carwash Google Business Profile, together with the reviewer’s public display name. They are used to present genuine customer feedback. The source is Google, and the excerpts remain visible while they are relevant and accurate.

A quoted reviewer can ask James Hand Carwash to correct or remove their public name or review excerpt by using the contact details below.

Links to Google

Directions, opening-hours and review buttons are links to Google. If you select one, your browser or device connects to Google and Google handles information under its own privacy terms. James Hand Carwash does not receive information about what you do on Google through these links.

Website images

The photographs displayed on the site are stored with the website, so viewing them does not connect your browser to a separate photography service.

Optional Google Map

The Google Map on the location section is not loaded automatically. It connects to Google only after you select “Load Google Map”. When loaded, Google may receive technical information such as your IP address and may use cookies or similar technologies under its own policies.

You can use the ordinary “Get Directions” link instead if you do not want to load the embedded map on this website.

For details, please read Google’s Privacy Policy.

Cookies and analytics

The website offers optional Google Analytics. It is disabled by default and its script is loaded only after you select “Accept” in the cookie panel. Selecting “Decline” leaves analytics disabled.

If accepted, Google Analytics may process technical and usage information such as pages viewed, approximate location, device and browser details, and referral information. It may set _ga and _ga_<container-id> cookies. The website is not configured to use analytics for advertising.

The site stores your “accepted” or “declined” choice in local storage under james_cookie_consent_v1. This preference is necessary to remember your decision. You can revisit your decision using “Cookie settings” in the footer.

Third-party cookies or similar technologies may also be used by Google if you separately choose to load the optional map or leave this website through a Google link. See the Cookie Policy for details.

Your data protection rights

Depending on the circumstances, UK data protection law may give you rights to ask for access to your personal information, correction, erasure, restriction of its use, or transfer of information you supplied.

You can also object to the use of your personal information where it is handled on the basis of legitimate interests.

Some rights are subject to legal conditions and exemptions. To make a request or raise a concern, contact James Hand Carwash using the details below.

If you remain unhappy after raising a concern with the business, you can find guidance on the Information Commissioner’s Office complaint page.
