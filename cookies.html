Cookie Policy

This page explains what the website stores, when optional Google Analytics can run and how to accept or decline it.

On this pageEssential preferenceGoogle AnalyticsOptional mapChange your choice

Essential preference storage

The site stores your cookie-panel decision in local storage under james_cookie_consent_v1. The value is either accepted or declined. This is necessary to remember your choice and prevent the panel from appearing on every page.

This preference does not track browsing activity and is not sent to James Hand Carwash as a cookie.

Optional Google Analytics

Google Analytics is disabled until you select “Accept”. If you decline, the analytics script is not requested and analytics cookies are not set by this website.

If accepted, Google Analytics can help the business understand which pages are visited, how visitors arrive, and general device or browser information. Google may receive technical information needed to provide the service.

Cookies used after acceptance

NamePurposeTypical duration
_gaDistinguishes site visitors for analytics reporting.Up to 2 years

No advertising or marketing cookies are configured by this website.

Optional Google Map

The embedded Google Map is separate from Analytics and is not loaded automatically. It connects to Google only if you select “Load Google Map”. Google may then use cookies or similar technologies under its own policies. You can use the ordinary directions link instead.

Read Google’s Privacy Policy for more about how Google handles information.

Change your choice

Select the button below or “Cookie settings” in any page footer. Choosing Decline removes accessible Google Analytics cookies and reloads the page if Analytics had already started.

You can also clear this site’s storage and cookies using your browser controls.
